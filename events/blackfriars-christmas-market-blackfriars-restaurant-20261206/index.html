Blackfriars' Christmas Market

Sunday 6th December

Blackfriars Restaurant

Friars Street, Newcastle upon Tyne, NE1 4XN

Get into the festive spirit with a visit to Blackfriars' Christmas market! The Cloister Garden will be transformed this winter, filled with local vendors to help you find the ideal, unique gift. Oh, and that's not forgetting that Blackfriars' own festive food and drink will be on-hand too, so there'll be plenty of homemade mulled wine and hot chocolate to keep you warm as you wander! Dates include: Saturday 5th DecemberSunday 6th December
