Polymer (Clay) Pumpkin Carving with Jed Buttress

Saturday 31st October, 11am

Cobalt Studios

10-16 Boyd Street, Shieldfield, Newcastle upon Tyne, NE2 1AP

In this seasonal session, participants will sculpt small pumpkin-inspired forms using polymer clay and add expressive carved faces and surface textures. The workshop introduces basic clay handling, simple carving tools – perfect for spooky Halloween, no prior sculpting experience needed. Please contact hello@creativeworkshopsnecic.com if you have any questions or access requirements. This workshop is delivered by Creative Workshops NE CIC, a not-for-profit company focusing on wellbeing outcomes through art. We serve communities that often feel excluded and would benefit from art practice, such as queer, neurodiverse and low-income groups. — Please note we are unable to offer refunds.
