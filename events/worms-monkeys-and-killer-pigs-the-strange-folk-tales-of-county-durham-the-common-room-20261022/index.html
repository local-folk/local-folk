Worms, Monkeys and Killer Pigs: The Strange Folk Tales of County Durham

Thursday 22nd October

The Common Room

Neville Hall, Westgate Road, Newcastle upon Tyne, NE1 1SE

Discover County Durham's quirky folk tales with this talk from author and storyteller Adam Bushnell at The Common Room. He'll be exploring some of its most interesting legends, from its famous worm tales and the dark history behind Hartlepool's monkey-hanging past to the county's alarming number of murderous pigs. He'll also unearth how stories change as they are passed to new generations and why such tales matter today, alongside telling a story or two himself.
