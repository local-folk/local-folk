Heritage Workshop

World Building & Map Making with Mark Bletcher

Saturday 28th November, 11am

Cobalt Studios

10-16 Boyd Street, Shieldfield, Newcastle upon Tyne, NE2 1AP

In this creative session, participants will explore world-building though map making as an art form. Using real and fantasy examples, participants will be given tools and a helpful structure to begin creating fantasy worlds through map-making. This session is for everyone interested in fantasy worlds, and can be used as a prop for collaborative games like D&D or solo writing. Please contact hello@creativeworkshopsnecic.com if you have any questions or access requirements. This workshop is delivered by Creative Workshops NE CIC, a not-for-profit company focusing on wellbeing outcomes through art. We serve communities that often feel excluded and would benefit from art practice, such as queer, neurodiverse and low-income groups. — Please note we are unable to offer refunds.
