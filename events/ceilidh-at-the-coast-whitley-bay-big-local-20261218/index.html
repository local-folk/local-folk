Ceilidh at the Coast

Friday 18th December, 7:30pm

Whitley Bay Big Local

305 Whitley Road, Whitley Bay, Tyne and Wear, NE26 2HU

An evening of ceilidh dancing at the Whitley Bay Big Local. The High Tide Ceilidh Band will provide high-energy music, alongside experienced caller, Chris Faulkner, who will explain every dance before it starts. Expect an evening packed with great live music, high energy dancing, and a warm atmosphere.
