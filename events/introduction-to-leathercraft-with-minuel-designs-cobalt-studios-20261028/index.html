Crafts

Introduction to Leathercraft with Minuel Designs

Wednesday 28th October, 6:30pm

Cobalt Studios

10-16 Boyd Street, Shieldfield, Newcastle upon Tyne, NE2 1AP

A fun evening of crafting, which will teach you the foundational skills for designing leather accessories and shoemaking. Through demonstrations and guided practice, you will learn how to work with leather, use professional tools and complete a small leather accessory from start to finish. This workshop is aimed at giving the community a strong foundation on the design of leather accessories and shoemaking techniques for beginners. Through demonstrations and guided practice, they will learn how to work with leather, use professional tools and complete a small leather accessory from start to finish. The workshop blends craft, creativity and fashion storytelling to enhance their inner creativity skills and broaden their understanding of material-based fashion practices. The workshop is facilitated by Emmanuel Faleti who, beyond crafting exceptional footwear, believes in leveraging his expertise and resources to create a lasting social impact. He is a shoemaker who excels not only in business but also strives to leave a lasting legacy that extends far beyond the soles of your shoes!
