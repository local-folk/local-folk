Made Up North - Winter Market

Sunday 6th December, 10am

Boiler Shop

20 South Street, Newcastle, NE1 3PE

Made Up North is hosting its Winter Market in the heart of Newcastle, bringing together 60 of the best northern creatives! Enjoy a drink from the BOILER SHOP’s in-house bar while viewing the incredible work of local potters, textiles artists, jewelers, soap makers, and so many other northern creatives. The first 15 people through the door will get a free gift! Tickets are £2 and can be bought online or at the door on the day of the market. Under 16s get free entry. 10% of all the profit from the ticket sale will be donated to the market's nominated charity.
